AGROPEER AI – Your Smart Farming Assistant 🌾

AGROPEER AI is a smart digital platform designed to support farmers with modern tools for agriculture management, crop trading, and community knowledge sharing. With AGROPEER AI, farmers can access useful information, collaborate with other farmers, and manage their farming activities more efficiently.

🌾 **Buy & Sell Agricultural Products**  
Connect with farmers, buyers, and sellers in your area. Easily list your crops for sale or discover agricultural products available nearby. AGROPEER AI simplifies crop trading and helps farmers find better opportunities in local markets.

Share your farming experiences, tips, and updates with a community of farmers. Ask questions, learn from other farmers, and build a collaborative agriculture network.

Track important farming activities in one place. Monitor crop information, trading activities, and market insights through a simple and easy-to-use dashboard.

Get instant AI-powered guidance for farming-related questions. Farmers can ask about crop management, pest issues, irrigation practices, and general agricultural topics to receive helpful suggestions. This is general guidance only and not a substitute for professional or government advice.
